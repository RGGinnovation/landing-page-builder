# Partner Landing Page

Input: the partner's link (their website, show, author page or social profile). A name is
optional. Example: "/partner-landing-page https://thepetekalinershow.com". Nothing else is
needed: the skill does the research, the writing and the checks, and hands back the JSON.

Output, both generated by `scripts/build.py` from one research file so they always match:

1. `<slug>.landing-page.json`: the final file to download. It holds the research, colors,
   sources, review status and the finished page (`page`). In the editor (/admin), open the
   partner's page and click **Fill from JSON**. It fills everything: the text, colors, theme,
   guide, call band, thank-you page, SEO, the partner's HubSpot form, and the photo and logo
   when the skill found them. The team adds only the Kiflo referral code, plus the photo or
   logo if none was found. To start a brand new page from the file instead, use Publish menu >
   Import JSON.
2. `<slug>-landing-page.md`: the same content to read and paste. It has every field in
   editor order (one code block per value), the colors with their readability checks, and
   every line of the finished page from top to bottom.

Everything the partner's audience sees that can be tailored is tailored to this partner alone:
- **Copy:** quote, signature, Why I Believe, 3 Reasons, call band, thank-you greeting,
  headline, message and note.
- **Page details:** photo, photo description, logo, their own brand colors, hero background,
  guide, page title and search description.

The rest is the fixed RGG template, and the sheet shows it so the reviewer sees the whole page.

Read these before writing anything:
- `references/research-playbook.md`: how to research a link in depth and verify identity.
- `references/field-spec.md`: every editor field, its rules and the fixed template parts.
- `references/voice-and-compliance.md`: the voice method and the compliance rules. They are
  absolute.
- `references/output-format.md`: the `input.json` you write, and what the JSON file contains.

## Workflow

### 1. Identify the partner from the link
First check whether they already have a page: open partner.revelationgoldgroup.com/<likely slug>.
If one is live, the kit is an update. Read the page, keep its slug and keep lines compliance
already approved, and say so in the reply.

Open the link. Work out the person, their show, ministry or brand, and their role. Confirm who
they are with at least two independent sources before you research further. Names and team
nicknames are ambiguous: A Sea of Red covers the Liberty University Flames, not the Calgary
Flames. Record the two sources in `partner.identityCheck`.

### 2. Research in depth (run these in parallel when you can)
Follow `research-playbook.md`:
- **The link's site:** About, bio, show, book, blog, press or media kit, and contact pages.
- **Public footprint:** socials, podcast pages, YouTube, Substack, interviews, Wikipedia, and
  local press.
- **HubSpot (read only), portal 44817109:**
  - Search contacts by name, domain and show.
  - Read every note on the contact.
  - Record the vanity domain (the contact's `website`), facts the partner shared, and any
    guidance in the notes. Guidance such as "let faith come from him"
    is binding.
- **The partner's HubSpot form:** find it, so the page captures leads with no manual step (see
  "HubSpot form" below).
- **Assets:** the best hero photo plus 2 alternates, a light or white logo, and the brand colors.
  Give direct image URLs; the editor copies them onto the page automatically.

When a host will not load, stop after two failed tries. Use search results and HubSpot instead,
and list the host under `blocked`. Never invent a fact to fill a gap. Leave the gap and say
what to get and from whom.

### HubSpot form
Each partner has their own form in portal 44817109. Put its id (a GUID) in
`fields.hubspotFormGuid`. The script builds the embed code from it, which is the same snippet
every live page uses. Record where it came from in `hubspot.form`:
`{ "guid", "name", "source" }`.

Look for it in this order:
1. **The forms list.** Use `manage_landing_page` with `action=FORMS`, or `manage_website_page`
   with operation `FORMS`. Match the form name to the partner's name, last name, show or slug
   (for example "Pete Kaliner Landing Page"). Prefer a published form, and the newest if several
   match. Never use a form that belongs to another partner or a generic RGG form. These tools
   need the HubSpot connector's forms read permission. If they refuse, say so once in the reply
   ("grant forms read access to the HubSpot connector to find forms automatically") and go to
   step 2.
2. **HubSpot notes.** Look in the contact's notes for a form id or link: a 36-character GUID
   near "form", `data-form-id="..."`, `js.hsforms.net` or `share.hsforms.com`.
3. **Not found.** Leave the field blank and the kit adds a to-do to create the form. If the
   partner already has a live page, Fill from JSON keeps that page's form.

**The Kiflo referral code is entered by the team.** Leave `kifloCode` blank unless a HubSpot
note or the company's `kiflo_*` properties state the exact code. If you do fill it, set
`kifloCodeConfirmed: true` and cite the source. Never propose or guess a code.

### 3. Verify
- **Claims:** every fact about the partner that appears in the copy goes in `claims`, with its
  source (a URL, or a HubSpot note and its date). A claim with no source comes out of the copy.
- **Experiences:** anything the copy says the partner did, owns or experienced (bought silver,
  chose or vetted RGG, their family's savings) is a claim with `kind: "experience"`, the exact
  `phrase` from the copy, and a source that is the partner's own words or a HubSpot note of what
  they told us. No such source: rewrite it as a belief or leave it out. The script blocks any
  first person action that no experience claim covers.
- **Figures:** every number in 3 Reasons goes in `figures`, checked today against the named
  source (national debt: fiscaldata.treasury.gov, Debt to the Penny). If the primary source
  will not load, a major outlet reporting the Treasury figure this month may stand in: put it in
  `figures` with that outlet as the source and add a to-do to check fiscaldata before launch.
  If you cannot verify a number at all, write the reason without it.
- **Names:** check company names, titles and show names against the official source. If
  sources disagree (for example a company name that differs between HubSpot and the website),
  add a to-do.

### 4. Decide, including their colors
Decide the slug, partner name, signature title, guide, hero background and thank-you layout by
the rules in `field-spec.md`, with a one-line reason each in `rationale`.

**Colors:** use the partner's own brand colors. Take them from their logo, their site's header,
buttons or `theme-color`, or their show artwork.
- Set `brandAccent`, their signature color, used for buttons, numbers and stars.
- Set `brandBand`, their darkest brand color, used for the top bar, hero, call band and footer.
- Use a preset only when no brand colors can be found, and add a to-do to confirm the colors.
- The script and the site check that every text and background pair is at least 4.5:1, so
  all text stays visible whatever colors are chosen.

### 5. Write every field
Follow `field-spec.md` and `voice-and-compliance.md`. Write the voice profile first, then the
copy.
- Every partner line is their personal opinion and belief only.
- It never says what gold and silver do, never implies a return and never advises the reader.
- It never puts words or actions in their mouth: no invented purchases, family choices, vetting
  stories or quotes. The one relationship fact is "I partnered with Revelation Gold Group".
- It never uses support, recommend, invest, sponsor, superlatives, unverified counts or reach,
  or hype (the full list is Rule 3 in `voice-and-compliance.md`).
- It uses only the approved company facts about Revelation Gold Group.
- It carries faith language only if the partner's own public frame is faith.

### 6. Build and fix until clean
Write `input.json` (see `output-format.md`), then run:

```bash
python scripts/build.py input.json <out_dir>
```

Fix every ERROR and rerun until it prints `COPY READY`.
- **WARN:** fix it or justify it.
- **TODO:** only a person can supply these (the HubSpot form, Kiflo confirmation, partner
  approval, photo upload). They stay in the file as the to-do list.

### 7. Deliver
The JSON file is the main deliverable. Always hand it over as a downloadable file: use a
file-sending tool if one exists, otherwise save it to the outputs folder and give its path.
Hand over the sheet the same way.

Then reply in 3 to 5 lines:
- The page link.
- Whether the copy is ready.
- "Open the page in /admin and click Fill from JSON, then upload the photo and logo."
- The open to-dos and anything uncertain.

## Quality bar
- The partner would recognize themselves: their words, rhythm and worldview, short and precise.
- Every metals sentence answers yes to one question: "Would the partner say this is their own
  belief?" Only then does it ship.
- Nothing invented. Every personal fact is sourced, every action or experience is confirmed by
  the partner, and every number is verified today. If a line would be false for this partner,
  it does not ship.
- House style: no em or en dashes, no exclamation
  marks in page copy.
- The page looks like the partner: their colors, their photo, their logo, their words.
- Every text is readable, with every color pair at 4.5:1 or more (the readability table in the
  sheet).
- The JSON imports cleanly. Its `page` passes the editor checklist apart from the human to-dos.
