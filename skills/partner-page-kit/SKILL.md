# Partner Page Kit

Input: a link (required) and optionally a name. Example: "Build the page kit for
https://thepetekalinershow.com".

Output, both generated by `scripts/build_kit.py` from one research file so they always match:

1. `<slug>.page-kit.json`: one downloadable file with the research, sources, review status and
   the import-ready page (`page`). The editor imports it as is: /admin > Publish menu >
   Import JSON.
2. `<slug>-page-kit.md`: the same content as a sheet to read and paste field by field, in
   editor order, with one code block per value.

Read these before writing anything:
- `references/research-playbook.md`: how to research a link in depth and verify identity.
- `references/field-spec.md`: every editor field, its rules and the fixed template parts.
- `references/voice-and-compliance.md`: the voice method and the compliance rules. They are
  absolute.
- `references/output-format.md`: the `kit-input.json` you write, and what the JSON file contains.

## Workflow

### 1. Identify the partner from the link
Open the link. Work out the person, their show, ministry or brand, and their role. Confirm who
they are with at least two independent sources before you research further. Names and team
nicknames are ambiguous: A Sea of Red covers the Liberty University Flames, not the Calgary
Flames. Record the two sources in `partner.identityCheck`.

### 2. Research in depth (run these in parallel when you can)
Follow `research-playbook.md`:
- **The link's site:** About, bio, show, book, blog, press or media kit, and contact pages.
- **Public footprint:** socials, podcast pages, YouTube, Substack, interviews, Wikipedia, and
  local press.
- **HubSpot (read only), portal 44817109:**
  - Search contacts by name, domain and show.
  - Read every note on the contact.
  - Record the vanity domain (the contact's `website`), any Kiflo code or form link, facts the
    partner shared, and any guidance in the notes. Guidance such as "let faith come from him"
    is binding.
- **Assets:** the best hero photo plus 2 alternates, a light or white logo, and the brand colors.

When a host will not load, stop after two failed tries. Use search results and HubSpot instead,
and list the host under `blocked`. Never invent a fact to fill a gap. Leave the gap and say
what to get and from whom.

### 3. Verify
- **Claims:** every fact about the partner that appears in the copy goes in `claims`, with its
  source (a URL, or a HubSpot note and its date). A claim with no source comes out of the copy.
- **Figures:** every number in 3 Reasons goes in `figures`, checked today against the named
  source (national debt: fiscaldata.treasury.gov, Debt to the Penny). If you cannot verify a
  number, write the reason without it.
- **Names:** check company names, titles and show names against the official source. If
  sources disagree (for example a company name that differs between HubSpot and the website),
  add a to-do.

### 4. Decide
Decide the slug, partner name, signature title, guide, theme preset, hero background and
thank-you layout by the rules in `field-spec.md`, with a one-line reason each in `rationale`.

### 5. Write every field
Follow `field-spec.md` and `voice-and-compliance.md`. Write the voice profile first, then the
copy.
- The quote, Why I Believe and 3 Reasons are the partner's personal opinion and choice only.
- They never say what gold and silver do.
- They never imply a return.
- They never advise the reader.
- They carry faith language only if the partner's own public frame is faith.

### 6. Build and fix until clean
Write `kit-input.json` (see `output-format.md`), then run:

```bash
python scripts/build_kit.py kit-input.json <out_dir>
```

Fix every ERROR and rerun until it prints `COPY READY`.
- **WARN:** fix it or justify it.
- **TODO:** only a person can supply these (the HubSpot form, Kiflo confirmation, partner
  approval, photo upload). They stay in the file as the to-do list.

### 7. Deliver
Give the user both files: send them if a file tool exists, otherwise save them and print the
sheet. Then reply in 3 to 5 lines: the page link, whether the copy is ready, the open to-dos,
and anything uncertain.

## Quality bar
- The partner would recognize themselves: their words, rhythm and worldview, short and precise.
- Every metals sentence answers yes to one question: "Is this what the partner believes or
  chose?" Only then does it ship.
- Nothing invented. Every personal fact is sourced, and every number is verified today.
- House style: no em or en dashes, no gold, yellow, amber, bronze or brass, no exclamation
  marks in page copy.
- The JSON imports cleanly. Its `page` passes the editor checklist apart from the human to-dos.
